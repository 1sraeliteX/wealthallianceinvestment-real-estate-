Overview

Property management refers to the administration, operation, and oversight of real estate properties on behalf of property owners. It involves various tasks such as marketing rental properties, finding tenants, collecting rent and ensuring legal compliance.

Property managers maintain accurate financial records, including income and expense tracking, budgeting, and providing owners with regular financial reports.

The Problem

Millions of Naira Lost to Poor Property Management

Property owners across Nigeria are losing millions of naira every year due to improper management of records and lack of accessibility to critical real estate documents.
